Objective:
Diagnose why expected Prometheus metrics are missing from the Azure Monitor workspace.

- Symptom reported by the customer
- Collected evidence (AKS, ama-metrics, DCR/DCE, connectivity, query)

1) Main hypothesis
- Explain the strongest likely cause using objective language.
- If there is not enough evidence, mark as "inconclusive".

2) Pipeline-stage triage
- Source metric exists?
- Target discovered?
- Target UP/DOWN?
- Scrape success?
- ama-metrics health?
- DCR/DCE associations correct?
- Connectivity/DNS/AMPLS ok?
- Ingestion/publication ok?
- Query/expectation correct?

3) Evidence supporting the hypothesis
- Cite concrete evidence with references to collected artifacts.

4) Probable failure domain
- Choose exactly 1 primary domain:
  Metric expectation | Target discovery | Scraping | ama-metrics | DCR/DCE | Connectivity | Ingestion/publication | Query/visualization | Outside Monitoring

5) Confidence level
- High / Medium / Low, with a short justification.

6) Best next step
- Single, executable action with owner (Customer / Support / Product).

7) Customer-ready message
- Short, professional, and direct text.

Rules:
- Do not infer root cause without evidence.
- Do not invent commands or results.
- If critical data is missing, explicitly state "missing evidence".
- Keep humans in control of the final decision.
